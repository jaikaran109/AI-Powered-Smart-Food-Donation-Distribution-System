Problem Definition

The Food Redistribution Bottleneck

Why traditional food rescue mechanisms fail to save cooked perishable surplus

01
Manual & Chaotic Coordination
Donors rely on phone calls, informal WhatsApp chats, or sporadic emails. Delays in communication cause response lag, missed dispatch windows, and frequent uncoordinated volunteer cancellations.
02
Perishability & Time-Window Failure
Cooked meals have a strict 4-to-6 hour consumption shelf life. Without dynamic urgency scoring and shelf-life modeling, food expires on kitchen counters before pickup logistics are established.
03
Geographic & Capacity Mismatches
Donors blindly call single NGOs that may lack volunteer drivers, vehicle space, or cold storage. A 200-meal batch gets dumped at a 30-bed shelter, leading to secondary spoilage while distant shelters starve.
04
Verification & Custody Security Void
Donors fear liability and reputational harm from unvetted collectors. Lack of cryptographic proof of delivery leads to untracked food diversions, loss of accountability, and zero audit trails for CSR.
Solution Highlights

Smart Food Donation Platform: The Solution

An end-to-end full-stack platform delivering intelligence, speed, and trust

Dynamic Urgency Scoring (0-100)
Real-time algorithmic calculation evaluating cooking timestamp, shelf-life baselines, and storage conditions (refrigerated, ambient, frozen). Automatically triggers visual badges ("Fresh", "Expiring Soon", "Emergency Dispatch") to prioritize courier routing.
AI Multi-Criteria Matchmaker (MCDA)
Calculates optimal NGO-donor pairings across 4 weighted vectors: Geospatial distance (Haversine formula), capacity-to-demand ratio, dietary safety alignment, and historical NGO trust ratings.
4-Stage Logistics & Secure Handover OTP
Transparent chain of custody: Pending ➔ Accepted ➔ Picked Up ➔ Delivered. Physical handover is sealed via a 6-digit cryptographic OTP, eliminating food diversion and fraud.
Interactive Maps & Control Tower
Full geospatial discovery via Leaflet OpenStreetMap with radial search filters. Administrators access real-time moderation, accreditation verification, and automated carbon offset calculations.

Logistics & Security

4-Stage Logistics Pipeline & 6-Digit OTP Handover

Securing the physical chain of custody with verified cryptographic transfer codes

STAGE 1

Pending Claim

Donor Action

Surplus batch listed with photos, dietary tag, and expiry deadline. AI ranks top 4 local NGOs and broadcasts instant alerts.
STAGE 2

Accepted & Claimed

NGO + Donor

NGO submits pickup request specifying vehicle mode, volunteer count, and beneficiary target. Donor confirms dispatch claim.
STAGE 3

Picked Up

Volunteer In-Transit

Volunteer arrives at donor kitchen. Donor checks credentials and system issues a tamper-proof 6-digit OTP code to the donor.
STAGE 4

Delivered

Verified Handover

NGO enters the secret OTP on their device. System verifies code cryptographically, seals the record, and increments impact KPIs.
🔐 Why 6-Digit Handover OTP Matters:
Eliminates counterfeit claims and food diversion. Protects donors from legal liability by recording verified custody transfer with an immutable timestamped audit log in MongoDB.

SHELF-LIFE ANALYTICS

Dynamic Freshness & Urgency (0-100)

  • Mathematical Decay Curve: Evaluates elapsed time from cooking against category-specific perishability baseline.
  • Storage Modifiers: Refrigeration factors extend safe window (+48 hrs); room-temp cooked items trigger high urgency within 4 hrs.
  • Automatic Escalation: When under 2 hours remain, urgency score climbs to 85+, notifying emergency response shelters.
  • Freshness Badges: Visual real-time indicators guide volunteers to the most time-critical listings first.

Database Design

Normalized Database Schema & Models

MongoDB Atlas cloud database structured across 8 optimized collections with geospatial indexing

CORE IDENTITY
User Collection
• role: ('donor' | 'receiver' | 'admin')
• organizationName, orgType, phone
• location: { type: "Point", coordinates: [lng, lat] }
• isVerified, verificationStatus
• metrics: { totalMealsSaved, rating, pickupsCompleted }
DONATIONS
FoodListing Collection
• donorId: ObjectId (ref: User)
• category, dietaryType, storageCondition
• quantity, unit, estimatedMeals
• cookedTime, expiryTime, urgencyScore (0-100)
• status: ('available' | 'reserved' | 'completed')
LOGISTICS
PickupRequest Collection
• listingId, donorId, receiverId
• transportType, volunteerCount, beneficiaries
• status: ('pending' | 'accepted' | 'picked_up' | 'delivered')
• verificationOtp: (6-digit secure code)
• timeline: [ { status, timestamp, note, updatedBy } ]
GOVERNANCE
Support Collections (5)
• ActivityLog: System audit events, actions & actor IDs
• Review: 5-star ratings, volunteer reviews & feedback
• Category: Food taxonomy classifications
• Notification: Unread alerts & dispatch signals
• Setting: Configurable system-wide parameters
